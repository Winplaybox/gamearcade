# Privacy Policy for Game Arcade (`com.winplaybox.gamearcade`)

**Effective Date:** July 31, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 31, 2026  
**Developer / Owner:** Winplaybox (`admin@winplaybox.com`)

Winplaybox ("we," "our," or "us") operates the **Game Arcade** mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Automatically Collected Information
When you access and play games within the App, we and our third-party service providers (such as Google AdMob and Firebase) may automatically collect certain non-personal diagnostic and usage information, including:
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, network state, and screen resolution.
- **Advertising Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (AAID) for ad personalization and frequency capping.
- **Usage Data:** App launch frequency, games played, category selections, screen views, and session duration.

### B. User-Submitted Information
- **Game Requests & Contact Form:** If you submit a game request or report a problem within the App, we collect the submitted game details, your name, and your email address solely to review the submission and communicate updates regarding your request.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- **Game Delivery:** To stream and render instant HTML5 games inside the embedded WebView player seamlessly.
- **Ad Serving & Analytics:** To deliver personalized and non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob and measure ad performance.
- **App Optimization:** To analyze crash logs, user preferences, and performance metrics via Firebase to improve stability and UX.
- **Communication:** To respond to user inquiries, support tickets, and game submissions.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The App integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** Serves banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.  
  *Privacy Policy:* [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google Firebase (Firestore & Analytics):** Manages dynamic game catalogs, crash diagnostics, and usage analytics.  
  *Privacy Policy:* [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## 4. Cookies & Web Storage

The instant HTML5 games loaded within the WebView player may use local storage, session storage, or cookies to save game progress, high scores, and sound settings locally on your device. We do not transmit or sell your local game progress data to external third parties.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Game Arcade is intended for a general audience (Ages 13 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete such data from our servers.

---

## 6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 7. Your Data Rights & Opt-Out Options

- **Data Deletion Requests:** You may request the deletion of any personal data submitted to us (such as contact information) by emailing us at **admin@winplaybox.com**.
